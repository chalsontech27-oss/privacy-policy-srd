Privacy Policy

SASSA R370 Status Check 2026

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Your privacy matters.

This application provides convenient access to the official SASSA SRD website. It is independently developed and is not an official government application.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SASSA R370 Status Check 2026 handles information when you use our Android application.

By using this application, you acknowledge that you have read this policy.

2. Information We Collect

The application is designed to provide access to the official SASSA SRD website.

We do not intentionally request or collect your South African identity number, banking details, grant application details, or passwords through a separate form in this application.

However, the official website and the services used to operate the application may process technical information, cookies, device information, or information you submit directly to them. The actual data processed depends on the website and the application's implementation.

3. Official SASSA Website

When you select the status-checking option, the application opens the official SASSA SRD website.

Information you submit on that website is handled by the website's operators under their applicable policies and practices. We do not control their data collection or processing activities.

Please review the information and privacy notices provided on the official website before submitting personal details.

Visit Official SASSA SRD Website

4. Cookies and Website Storage

The official website may use cookies, browser storage, or similar technologies to support its functionality. If the website is displayed inside the application, these technologies may operate within its web browser component.

Their use is governed by the applicable website practices.

5. Information Sharing

We do not intentionally sell personal information collected through a separate in-app data collection process.

When you access the official SASSA website, information you submit may be transmitted to that website and processed by its operators. Any third-party services integrated into the application may also process information according to their functions and applicable policies.

6. Data Security

We aim to use appropriate safeguards in the application. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Do not share your passwords, PINs, or sensitive personal information with anyone claiming to represent this app.

7. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for people seeking information about SRD grants. We do not intentionally design a separate process to collect personal information from children.

8. External Websites

The application provides access to an external government website. We are not responsible for the content, security, privacy practices, or availability of external websites.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application or its data practices change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

Please review this page periodically for changes.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the application, contact the developer using the support contact provided on the application's Google Play listing.

Developer: SASSA R370 Status Check 2026

11. Government Disclaimer

SASSA R370 Status Check 2026 is an independent application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the South African Social Security Agency (SASSA) or any South African government department.

The official government website is: srd.sassa.gov.za
